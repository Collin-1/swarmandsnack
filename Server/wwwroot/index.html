How to Play

  • Objective: Free-for-all with up to 8 players — eat everyone else's swarm before yours is gone!
  • Lobby: Players join with the room code, then the host presses Start Match.
  • Controls: Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move your Leader.
  • The World: Every player starts in their own room of a larger world — the camera follows your Leader as you explore. With 4 or fewer players, only half the world is open.
  • Mechanics: Your Leader (big circle) eats any rival dots it touches. Walls block movement — use doorways to raid other rooms or escape.
  • Winning: If you run out of underlings, you're out. The last player with a swarm wins!
